ClockWork

Co-founder & CEO

Attendance, payroll and advances for the people who run India's homes, shops and small teams — a business still largely run on paper registers and memory. Employers use a web portal; their staff get the same record free on their phone, in nine Indian languages.

The hard part is trust, not arithmetic. I designed a dual ledger where employer and worker see identical records, and where an unmarked day is disclosed rather than quietly deducted, so neither side has to take the other's word for it.

Built as a TypeScript monorepo on AWS. I develop almost entirely with AI agent tooling — Claude Code, custom MCP servers, headless Playwright harnesses — including adversarial multi-agent test fleets that simulate months of real use and have caught around thirty genuine defects, among them wage-calculation and session-isolation bugs.
